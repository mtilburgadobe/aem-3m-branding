# Product
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users
Technical buyers and customers seeking 3M products and industry solutions; secondary audiences seeking corporate news, careers, investment and regulatory information.
Scene: a buyer arrives at work on desktop, or checks a solution on a phone, and needs a useful next step without decoding a corporate campaign.

## Product Purpose
Make the practical value of 3M science clear and help each visitor reach the right product, industry or company destination.
Current scope: redesign the US-English homepage as a reviewable prototype. AEM implementation requires subsequent user approval.

## Positioning
3M connects material science to the needs of industry and everyday life.
Preserve the captured headline and real examples; do not add unverified commercial claims.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Retain product search, Brands, ten industry panels, all six captured news stories, Ask 3M referral, company links and the complete help/regulatory/legal footer.
Homepage only. No crawling or rebuilding linked destinations. External journeys go to their captured source URLs.
September 2026 visual identity PDF is normative and overrides legacy website styling.
Use unchanged official logo artwork from the supplied PDF. Licensed-font availability is unverified: use the PDF-permitted Arial/Helvetica system fallback, not a substitute brand font.
No invented metrics, quotes, product claims, dates, testimonials or AI-assistant functionality.
No analytics or consent tracking in the prototype.

## Brand Commitments
Register: brand. Personality: genuine, imaginative, insightful, science-led and confidently restrained.
Lead with 3M red; simple is smart; show and tell.
Redesign, not rebrand: preserve identity and customer task priority while replacing the old composition.
Anti-references: generic SaaS hero, glass panels, decorative gradients, excessive colored surfaces, rounded-card walls, fabricated science graphics, editorial cosplay, inaccessible photo overlays.

## Evidence on Hand
`stardust/current/pages/index.json` and rendered HTML contain live homepage content and destinations.
Source photographs, video, logo, favicon and font evidence are captured locally. The font evidence is not licensed for redistribution by this task.
The supplied PDF contains identity, typography, color, imagery, grid, logo and element rules.
No new product-detail pages, search service, authenticated account or assistant backend is built here.

## Product Principles
**Preserve discovery.** Product search and industry routing remain immediately findable.
**Show science in context.** Carry the original science film, industry photos and real news.
**Simplify the decision.** Improve hierarchy and reduce repeated visual weight without removing evidence.
**Keep the brand whole.** Use the PDF consistently, including in browser states and mobile composition.
**Be honest about delivery.** Link to existing services; distinguish prototype approval from AEM publishing.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Keyboard-operable menus and industry selectors; visible focus; meaningful image labels; readable text contrast; no horizontal overflow.
The original video must have a real still poster, a pause control and an intentional reduced-motion state.
