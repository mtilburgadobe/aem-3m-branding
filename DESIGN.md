# Design System: 3M

## Overview
**Creative North Star: "Possibility made clear."**
The supplied identity system, translated into a precise, contemporary web experience. Strong hierarchy, genuine scientific imagery and a flexible three-column grid carry the expression. Red earns attention rather than filling every available surface.
Density is balanced and compact. Every system decision serves clarity or recognition, not decoration.

## Colors
3M red is exactly `#D10411`, including actions and purposeful rectangular elements. Use pure black and white as required by the PDF.
Body and legal text are black on white or white on red. Gray is structural, not a third text color. Red type is limited to short labels/emphasis with measured contrast. No blue legacy links or functional-palette business identities.
Rosewood is not needed in this prototype. Do not pair it directly with 3M red.

## Typography
3M Circular is the preferred identity typeface. The PDF explicitly permits Arial, Helvetica or San Francisco for web/screen; this task uses Arial with Helvetica/sans-serif fallbacks because licensing has not been verified.
Bold, sentence-case, left-aligned headings. Headline leading is 1.1; tracking does not tighten beyond -0.005em. No gradients, underline effects or unnecessary mixed weights in headlines.
Body copy is regular for comfortable reading, with short prominent statements allowed in bold. Reading measure caps at 70ch. Small footer copy is at least 15px.
The hierarchy progresses in visible steps; individual sizes are rounded to practical web values. No new variable family is introduced to satisfy a tool rule.

## Layout
Three equal columns form the underlying grid; compositions may span one, two or three. Use a 1344px maximum container, derived from three 416px columns with two 48px gutters. At 1440px the outer margins are 48px.
Default section padding is 64px desktop, 48px tablet, 32px phone. Phone margins are 24px.
Logo/lockup stays top-left inside the margins, with preferred clear space at least half the height of the 3M mark. Keep the lockup above the PDF's 19px screen minimum and the logo above 13px.
The compact horizontal header uses the digital-grid relationship between mark height and margin (PDF p71); the entire lockup's aspect ratio never changes.
Mobile collapses navigation and stacks compositions. The industry selector becomes a labeled select control, not tiny wrapping tabs.

## Elevation & Depth
Flat by default. Typography, white space, photography and thin neutral rules separate content; no card shadows, blur or faux depth.

## Shapes
Squares, rectangles and bars only as meaningful identity elements. Zero corner radius for authored controls and panels. No phased-out triangle, decorative badge or recreated logo container.
The source photographic texture remains inside a meaningful company image role, not as a fabricated grain overlay.
